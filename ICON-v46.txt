完整生成提示词
Create one finished app icon artwork for an elegant Chinese daily practice and study app, not a mockup.
Square 1:1 canvas, fully opaque warm ivory background #f7f3e7 covering every pixel to the edges. NO rounded outer square, NO outside shadow, NO device frame.
A distinctive, beautifully balanced miniature shanshui emblem: two broad organic mountain silhouettes in pale celadon and muted sage green, a small warm pale-gold moon high on the right, and one ivory winding river cut through the lower landscape. Compose as a single centered softly circular medallion, with calm generous ivory negative space around it. All meaningful painted elements entirely within the centered 70% of the square so a circular app mask never cuts them. Mountains have soft flowing Chinese ink-brush contours with very restrained watercolor texture, clean bold silhouettes readable at 32 pixels. Largest foreground mountain medium-light sage #809879, distant mountain pale celadon #bdcbb5, moon #d5be85. Subtly hand-painted, contemporary cultured Chinese aesthetic, a refined small mark that could be printed on a beautiful book cover. Flat, quiet and luminous, not childish.
Critical: an ICON with very few large shapes and high clarity, NOT a detailed landscape painting. No trees, buildings, birds, people, animals, sheep, mascots, text, Chinese characters, letters, calligraphy, red stamp, black ink, dark forest green field, tiny decorative strokes, gradients with muddy colors, glossy 3D effects, photographic objects, or multiple alternatives.
